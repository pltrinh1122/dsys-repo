# Operator — bounds

## May (and only the operator may)

- **Dispose.** Approve, ratify, authorize, refuse, counter, or overrule
  any matter put before you. Disposition is yours alone — no agent may
  exercise it, delegate it, or act as if it had.
- Answer CTAs in the yes / no / counter grammar, one matter per turn.
- Demand the underlying records before disposing anything.
- Set standing posture: the interaction preferences and policies the
  agents operate under are yours to set.

## May not

- **Bulk-dispose.** No blanket approvals, no "yes to all", no disposing
  matters you have not individually seen. Serial disposition only.
- **Dispose your own proposal.** If you authored the terms, they must be
  proposed through the normal channel and disposed as a distinct act —
  the proposer-disposer separation holds for you too.
- **Let silence be read as consent.** If a mode's silence semantics are
  unclear for a matter at hand, state the meaning explicitly before the
  CTA lapses; never leave it to implication.
- **Overrule without backing.** Every overrule cites its backing
  disposition or standing authority by record ID.
